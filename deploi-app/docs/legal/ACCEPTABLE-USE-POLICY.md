This policy applies to everyone who uses the Deploi Service and to every Agent configured in it. It is
part of the Terms of Service. "You" includes the people you let use your account and the Agents you
authorize: **an Agent acting within your Authorization is acting for you.**

## 1. Accounts and stores you may connect

You may connect only stores, CRM portals, calendars and mailboxes that your business owns or is
authorized in writing to manage. Do not connect a competitor's or a former client's account, or keep an
account connected after your authority to manage it ends.

## 2. Prohibited content and conduct

You must not use the Service, or instruct an Agent, to:

1. **Send unsolicited or unlawful e-mail**: bulk or marketing e-mail to people who have not consented
   where consent is required; messages without a working unsubscribe where one is required; messages
   with false or misleading headers, sender identity or subject lines; mail to purchased, rented,
   scraped or harvested lists; or mail designed to evade spam filters. (See the AI Agent Authorization
   Terms, section 7, for your e-mail responsibilities.)
2. **Deceive buyers**: publish false product claims, fake scarcity or countdowns, fake or incentivized
   reviews presented as genuine, "was/now" prices that were never charged, or prices and discounts that
   breach consumer-protection or price-display law.
3. **Impersonate** a person, brand or organization, or present an Agent's message as written personally
   by a named human when it was not, where that would mislead the recipient. [COUNSEL: AI-disclosure
   rules — REVIEW-CHECKLIST A-4.]
4. **Sell or promote prohibited goods**: anything illegal where it is sold or delivered, or prohibited by
   the platform you sell on (for example Shopify's Acceptable Use Policy).
5. **Harass, threaten, discriminate** against, or collect data about individuals beyond what the
   business task needs; process special-category data (health, biometrics, sexual orientation, etc.) or
   children's data through Agents.
6. **Monitor competitors unlawfully**: the Competitor & Trend Watcher reads only public pages, obeys
   robots.txt and rate limits, and never signs in. Do not use it, or any Agent, to access non-public
   pages, bypass blocks, CAPTCHAs or paywalls, or collect personal data.
7. **Attack the Service or others**: probe, scan or test the Service's vulnerabilities without our
   written permission (report issues to `security@getdeploi.com` instead); interfere with other
   customers; exceed rate limits; use another organization's data; reverse-engineer the Service except
   as law permits; or use it to build a competing product.
8. **Abuse platforms**: use the Service in a way that breaches the terms of Shopify, WooCommerce,
   HubSpot, Google, Microsoft, Stripe or any connected platform, or that could get Deploi's app
   suspended there.
9. **Misuse prompts and files**: place instructions in product descriptions, uploaded files, e-mails or
   other content intended to make an Agent exceed its Authorization. (Agents treat such content as data,
   not as instructions, but attempting it is still a breach.)
10. **Infringe rights**: upload or have Agents publish content that infringes intellectual property,
    privacy or publicity rights.

## 3. Limits and fair use

Plan limits (Agents, actions, e-mails, competitor stores, seats, connections) are enforced by the Service.
Circumventing them, for example by creating several organizations for one business, is a breach.

## 4. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected breaches, and may remove content, pause an Agent, revoke its address,
suspend a connection or suspend the account, proportionately to the risk and, where possible, after
notice. Severe or repeated breaches may lead to termination. We may report illegal activity to the
authorities and cooperate with platforms.

## 5. Reporting

Report abuse (for example spam from an agent address) to `abuse@getdeploi.com` [CONFIRM]. Include the
message headers when reporting e-mail.
