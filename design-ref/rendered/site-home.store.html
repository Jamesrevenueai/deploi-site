Questions, answered.

How is Deploi different from Shopify Sidekick?+

Sidekick is a great free assistant that helps when you ask it. Deploi agents work on their own: they watch your store all day, take care of jobs end to end, and keep a proof log of everything they did.

Can an agent do something I did not want?+

Every agent starts with the safest permissions. Anything that spends money, changes prices or goes outside your rules waits for your approval.

What counts as an action?+

One finished step — an email sent, an order updated, a product rewritten, an invoice drafted. Watching your store and checking for problems is free.

What happens if I run out of actions?+

Agents keep working and extra actions are billed at $5 per 100, up to the monthly cap you set. Hit the cap and agents pause anything non-urgent until next month.

Does Deploi Office work without Shopify?+

Yes. Office is its own product. It connects to Outlook, your CRM and QuickBooks, and works for home services, property managers, leasing and real estate agents.
